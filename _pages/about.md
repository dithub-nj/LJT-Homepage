# About Me

I am Junteng Liu, a first‑year PhD candidate in the [NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He.

## Academic Background
- **2024‑Present**: Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)
- **2020‑2024**: B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). Graduated in June 2024. Awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

## Research Interests
My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:
1. LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
2. Hallucination in Vision‑Language Models (VLM)
3. LLM truthfulness and Interpretability

## Research Experience
- **February 2025 – Present**: Research Intern, MINIMAX
- **June 2024 – September 2024**: Research Intern, Tencent WXG (Advisor: Zifei Shan)
- **June 2023 – December 2023**: Research Intern, Shanghai AI Lab (Advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng)
